# Google Business Profile Setup Checklist — Moscool Technical Services

The SEO scan identified the absence of a Google Business Profile (GBP) as the
single biggest missed local-SEO opportunity: local service searches dominate in
Nigeria and Google Maps results drive the majority of calls. A verified,
photo-rich profile is what gets the business into the "map pack" — the 3
businesses shown with a map when someone searches "AC repair near me".

Time required: ~60–90 minutes for setup + a few days for the verification step.
Cost: free.

---

## 1. Create the profile

- [ ] Go to https://business.google.com and sign in with a Gmail account you
      control (use the business's own account, not a personal one staff can't
      reach later)
- [ ] Choose **Add a business** → enter the exact name:
      **Moscool Technical Services**
      - Do NOT add keywords like "AC Repair" to the name — that violates Google
        guidelines and can get the profile suspended
- [ ] Choose the business type: **Local store / service area business**

## 2. Categories (most important field after the name)

- [ ] Primary category: **HVAC Contractor** (highest search volume for your
      services; you can switch to Air Conditioning Repair Service later and
      compare)
- [ ] Secondary categories (add all that apply):
      - Air Conditioning Repair Service
      - Solar Energy Equipment Supplier
      - Electrician
      - Refrigeration Contractor (if offered in the category list)

## 3. Location & service area

- [ ] Address: enter your real business address in Lagos Mainland. If you work
      from home / don't want a public storefront, tick **"I deliver goods and
      services to my customers"** and clear "Show my address" so the profile
      shows your service area instead
- [ ] Service areas (add as many as you genuinely serve — the website now
      targets these):
      - Lagos Mainland (and specific areas: Ikeja, Yaba, Surulere, Maryland,
        Gbagada, Ogba)
      - Ogun State (Magboro, Mowe, Ibafo, Abeokuta if served)
- [ ] Match the website: your site now says "Lagos Mainland & Ogun State" —
      keep both profiles consistent

## 4. Contact details (must match the website exactly)

- [ ] Phone: **+234 903 315 0460** (same number as the website so Google
      correlates the signals)
- [ ] Website: your site URL (e.g. `https://moscooltech.onrender.com` or your
      custom domain if you set SITE_URL)
- [ ] WhatsApp: add it in the "Chat" / messaging section if available
- [ ] Hours:
      - Mon–Fri: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
      - Sat–Sun: mark as "Open 24 hours" is NOT accurate — leave closed but
        add "24/7 emergency call-out" in the description and via attributes if
        available

## 5. Description (750 characters max)

- [ ] Paste and adapt this draft:

> Moscool Technical Services provides expert AC repair, installation and
> servicing, commercial and home refrigeration, solar panel and inverter
> installation, and electrical work for homes and businesses across Lagos
> Mainland and Ogun State, Nigeria. With 15+ years of experience, our licensed
> technicians handle everything from split-unit gas recharges to full 5kVA+
> solar and lithium power systems. We offer 24/7 emergency response for
> refrigeration breakdowns and AC faults. Call or WhatsApp +234 903 315 0460
> for a same-day quote.

## 6. Photos (the scan said "photo-rich" — this matters a lot)

Target: **at least 10 real photos before asking for reviews.**

- [ ] Logo (square) and a cover image (your best van/team/installation shot)
- [ ] Real job photos (not stock): AC units you installed, solar panel arrays
      on roofs, freezer/compressor repairs, electrical panels
- [ ] Team/technician at work — faces build trust
- [ ] Replace the stock placeholder images currently on the website portfolio
      with these same real photos (consistency between site and profile)

## 7. Products / Services section

- [ ] Add services matching the website: AC Repair, AC Installation,
      Refrigeration Repair, Solar Panel Installation, Inverter Installation,
      Electrical Wiring, Preventive Maintenance
- [ ] Give each a short description and price-from (e.g. "AC service from
      ₦XX,XXX") — pricing transparency converts searchers into callers

## 8. Verification

- [ ] Request verification (usually postcard by mail to the address, or video
      verification in Nigeria). Follow the instructions in the dashboard
- [ ] A postcard code typically arrives within 5–14 days; enter it in the
      dashboard to go live
- [ ] Do not change the business name or address while verification is pending

## 9. After verification — the review engine

The scan recommends 20+ reviews within 90 days to build map-pack authority:

- [ ] After every completed job, send this WhatsApp message (copy-paste):

> Thank you for choosing Moscool Technical Services! 🙏 If you were happy with
> the work, could you leave us a quick Google review? It really helps our small
> business: [YOUR-REVIEW-LINK]

- [ ] Get your review link: in the GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy the
      short link (format: `g.page/r/XXXX/review`)
- [ ] Ask every customer — target 2 per week = 24+ in 90 days
- [ ] Reply to every review (positive or negative) within 48 hours; replies
      signal activity to Google

## 10. Keep both in sync (monthly, 5 minutes)

- [ ] Post 1–2 GBP "Updates" per month — reuse the website news/portfolio
      content (e.g. the Ikeja AC repair or Magboro solar case studies already
      seeded on the site)
- [ ] When you add a portfolio post on the website, add the same photos to GBP
- [ ] Check GBP "Performance" → see which queries call you, and consider adding
      website pages for the top ones

---

## Why this is worth the hour

| Signal | Effect |
|---|---|
| Verified profile with matching name/phone/website (NAP) | Google trusts and ranks the business for "near me" searches in Lagos/Ogun |
| Correct category (HVAC Contractor) | Eligible for the map pack for AC/repair queries |
| 10+ real photos | Profiles with photos get ~40%+ more direction requests and calls |
| 20+ reviews in 90 days | Map-pack ranking factor #1 after relevance and distance |
| Consistent location wording with the website | Reinforces the LocalBusiness schema + areaServed already added to the site |

The website side of local SEO (location in title/H1/schema/footer, portfolio
case studies, keyword-targeted content) is already done in code — the profile
is the missing half that makes Google surface it on Maps.
