AC, Refrigeration & Solar Services in Lagos Mainland & Ogun State

Moscool Technical Services is a Lagos-based technical team covering air conditioning, commercial and domestic refrigeration, solar and inverter systems, and electrical installation. Whether you need an AC repaired in Ikeja, a cold room brought back to temperature in Ogun State, or a solar system designed for a home in Magboro, the same process applies: diagnose properly, quote in writing, fix it once, and stand behind the work.

What we are called out for most often

Air conditioners that run but no longer cool, fridges and freezers that stop holding temperature, tripping breakers, and inverters that shut down under load. In Lagos these faults are usually caused by the same handful of things: dust-clogged condenser coils, refrigerant leaks, failed capacitors, corroded outdoor units near the coast, and battery banks that were undersized from day one. We test before we quote, so you are not paying for a guess.

How our pricing works

Call-outs are quoted upfront and most repairs are priced per fault, not per hour, so the cost does not creep while the technician is on site. Typical ranges are published in our guides — AC servicing from about ₦10,000 per unit, refrigerant recharges from about ₦25,000, and full solar packages from about ₦1.2m for a 1.5kVA essentials system. Read the full guides and pricing breakdowns before you book, or ask for a written quote on +234 903 315 0460.
