How this protects quality

  • Drafts are created unpublished by default, so nothing goes live without a human review.
  • The prompt is grounded in your real services, service areas and Naira price ranges — and forbids invented certifications or statistics.
  • Generated text is plain text rendered through the escaped article_html filter; it cannot inject HTML.
  • Every successful article gets FAQ schema and a unique slug automatically.
  • All calls are admin-only, CSRF-protected and throttled to 6 per 10 minutes per user.
